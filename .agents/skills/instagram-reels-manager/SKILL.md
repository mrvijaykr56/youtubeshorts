# Instagram Reels Manager & Viral Cross-Posting Skill 📱✨

This skill orchestrates the multi-platform expansion of **Magical Tales Kidz** across Instagram Reels ([@magicaltaleskid](https://www.instagram.com/magicaltaleskid/)). It ensures that every 3D animated Short produced for YouTube is seamlessly adapted, packaged, and optimized for maximum organic reach and explore page visibility on Instagram.

---

## 1. Instagram Reels Algorithm Mechanics 🧠

Instagram Reels operates on a multi-stage recommendation and indexing system:
1. **Watch Time & Completion Rate**: Watching a 30–40s reel to completion (or looping) signals algorithmic quality.
2. **Saves & Shares > Likes**: The algorithm weights **Shares** (DM sends between parents) and **Saves** (bookmarking stories for bedtime) 5x higher than passive likes.
3. **The 3-Second Retention Rule**: The first 1.5–3 seconds determine whether a non-follower in the Explore/Reels tab swipes away. Dynamic visual motion and bold Devanagari text overlays are mandatory in frame 1.
4. **First 120 Characters (Above the Fold)**: Mobile captions truncate at ~120 characters. The curiosity hook question and emojis must fit completely before the user clicks "...more" (Devanagari characters count the same as Latin characters).
5. **Multimodal SEO (Text + Speech + OCR)**: Instagram's AI indexes spoken audio, on-screen text, caption keywords, and display name. Keywords like `"Hindi Kahaniya"`, `"Panchatantra Stories"`, and `"Bal Kahaniya"` directly rank the post in search.

## 2. Anti-Cannibalization Cadence & Publishing Rules ⏱️

Empirical audit data from October 2026 revealed that **batch-uploading destroys algorithmic distribution**:
- On Oct 21, 2026, dropping 8 Reels within a 40-minute window choked the seed distribution engine, yielding single-digit views on 3 videos.
- Conversely, single isolated drops (e.g. *Jadui Chakki*) surged to **381 plays and 15 likes (+433% above median)**.

### Strict Publishing Protocol:
- **Daily Frequency**: Exactly **1 Reel per day**.
- **Optimal Time**: **18:30 IST (6:30 PM Indian Standard Time)** to capture peak evening family relaxation and bedtime co-viewing.
- **Minimum Interval**: Never publish two Reels within 12 hours of each other.
- **Cross-Post Timing**: Post to Instagram Reels **at the exact same time or 30 minutes after** the YouTube Shorts drop to synchronize multi-platform social proof.

## 4. Technical Specifications & Safe Margins 📐

Every reel published to `@magicaltaleskid` MUST adhere to these technical standards:
- **Aspect Ratio**: 9:16 vertical (1080x1920 pixels).
- **Duration**: 28 to 40 seconds (optimal for loop completion).
- **Codec**: H.264 video, AAC audio at 48kHz stereo.
- **Safe Margins & Subtitle Placement**:
  - Keep visual focus (character faces, main action) inside the central 1080x1350 area.
  - Subtitles must be centered with a **safe bottom margin of 280–320px** so Instagram's username, caption overlay, and audio attribution badge don't cover the words.
- **Cover Photo (Thumbnail)**: 9:16 high-contrast frame with bold emotional character expression.
- **AI-Content Labeling**: For accounts where Meta displays the "AI info" toggle, toggle the **"Made with AI"** label on video upload to ensure full compliance with Meta's transparency standards.

## 7. Operational Risk Warning & Automation Tooling ⚠️🛠️

### Operational Risk Warning (`instagrapi`):
`instagrapi` is an unofficial Instagram private API wrapper. To prevent account challenges, checkpoints, or temporary shadowbans:
- Maintain random human-like delays (2 to 5 seconds) between calls (`cl.delay_range = [2, 5]`).
- Never perform rapid bulk uploads (always 1 reel at a time).
- Keep session tokens cached in `backend/instagram_session.json` to prevent repeated fresh logins from triggering security alerts.

### Automated Tooling (`backend/scripts/`):
